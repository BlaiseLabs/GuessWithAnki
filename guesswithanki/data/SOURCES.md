kanji.json and readings.json are derived from KANJIDIC2 and JMdict, made by the Electronic Dictionary Research and
Development Group (EDRDG, https://www.edrdg.org/), used under the Creative Commons Attribution-ShareAlike 4.0
licence (https://www.edrdg.org/edrdg/licence.html). These derived files are shared under the same licence.
kanji.json: each kanji's English meanings, on'yomi, kun'yomi, newspaper frequency rank and school grade.
readings.json: for each kanji, how many common JMdict words read it each way (split by GuessWithAnki's aligner).

hanzi.json and pinyin.json are derived from CC-CEDICT (https://cc-cedict.org/), used under the Creative Commons
Attribution-ShareAlike 4.0 licence; these derived files are shared under the same licence.
hanzi.json: for each Chinese character, its English meanings (from the dictionary's one-character entries) and how many
dictionary words read it each way (pinyin syllable + tone). pinyin.json: the pinyin syllables.

morphemes_english.json and morphemes_medical.json: word parts and short meanings, as a language model (gpt-5.6-terra)
gave them when labelling the decks the README measures (a part is kept when two or more words used it). They hold no
deck text: each entry is a part and a few words of meaning.
